I specialize in understanding game systems at a deep technical level through reverse engineering, experimentation, simulation, and performance analysis. My work spans gameplay optimization, input analysis, custom hardware/software tooling, and the study of mechanics, balance, and player-facing behavior.

Much of that work grew out of long experience in speedrunning, where optimization, systems thinking, and rigorous testing are essential. Over time, I have applied that mindset to broader technical projects, public research, and game-focused tools.
